Research

Statistical process monitoring, quality engineering & intelligent analytics

Research spanning control-chart design, nonparametric monitoring, robust statistics, adaptive sampling, Industry 4.0 applications, artificial intelligence and data-driven decision systems.

Research themes

Core directions

Statistical Process Control

Design and performance analysis of control-chart schemes, including group-runs, EWMA, run-sum, double-sampling and adaptive monitoring methods.

Distribution-Free & Robust Methods

Nonparametric and robust monitoring methods for location, scale, coefficients of variation and complex process distributions.

AI & Advanced Analytics

Data science, machine learning, deep learning, image processing and intelligent applications for industrial and decision-support problems.
